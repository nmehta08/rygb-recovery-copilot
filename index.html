Clinical Story

A concise longitudinal reconstruction of this patient's recovery.
The patient underwent laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass 12 days ago. The procedure was completed without a documented intraoperative complication. Early postoperative pain and nausea initially improved.

Weight loss is progressing and there is no recorded fever or sustained tachycardia. The patient's course remains broadly consistent with expected early recovery.

Over the last several days, however, fluid intake has declined while nausea has increased. The patient now reports intermittent dizziness and constipation. These changes represent a shift from the prior improving trajectory and merit focused clinical reassessment.
Emerging change
The key finding is not simply that nausea is present. Nausea had been improving and has now worsened alongside reduced fluid intake. The direction of change is clinically more informative than an isolated symptom.
